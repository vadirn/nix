# Weekly status

Use it once a week per Sponsor or Accountable person. Rules: A3, A5, A8.

```
<name>, status for <work>:

- Done: <what changed, in the reader's currency>
- Next: <the next step>
- Blocked: <what blocks me and who can unblock it, or "nothing">
- Risk: <what may move the target, or "none">
- Confidence in <target and date>: <high | medium | low>, because <one reason>

Next update: <day and time>.
```

For invisible work, the "Done" line says what the next tasks would cost without it.
